About these ratings

+14 beats -14 by four touchdowns on a neutral field. The whole division fits in 52 points. Scoring is the same idea for the total — how many points this team adds to a game, whichever sideline it is on. Ratings come out of the same model that prices Saturday, and carry the same caveats. Every build is archived; a Move column and a change-since chooser appear with the second one.
