How to read this page

GS Proj is this site's own projection for the week: each player's season projection spread over his school's games, tilted by what the game model expects of this week's game, and zero on a bye. Pts is the points actually scored so far, as Yahoo scores them, with the stat line behind them. Yahoo projects a team total but no player-by-player number for the college game, so the player column is ours alone; a player past the board's depth shows — there but still plays. While games are on, points, stat lines and Yahoo's win odds refresh in place about once a minute. Ahead of kickoff a roster whose bench out-projects a starter gets the swap spelled out under the table. Rebuilt several times a day (last: Sep 6, 1:01 PM EDT); finished weeks stay on record. Standings and waivers are on the league dashboard, season-long roster strength on the power rankings.
