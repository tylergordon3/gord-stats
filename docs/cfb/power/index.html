About these rankings

FPI is expected point margin against an average FBS team on a neutral field; the projected record is ESPN's simulation of each team's actual schedule. Preseason these are projections; once games are played the same numbers update with results. GordStats is the same idea from this site's own model, the rating the predictions run on, with its rank beside it; sort by it for our order, and Move then counts places climbed in our ranking. Move is the change in whichever column the table is sorted by - places climbed in the FPI rank until you sort by another, then that figure's change - since the point the buttons pick. Every build is archived, so the choice runs from the last build (Sep 12) back to the season's first, or to the end of any week's games.

Rating is the ratings, the projected record and what the schedule has been worth; Odds what ESPN's simulations give each team. Click a figure's header to sort by it; click again to reverse. Top 25 highlighted - the highlight follows the team, so the FPI top 25 stay marked however the table is sorted. SOS is strength-of-schedule rank (hardest first) and Rem SOS the same for the games still to play; SOR is strength of record - where an average top-25 team would sit with this resume - and GC game control, the share of game time spent in the lead.
