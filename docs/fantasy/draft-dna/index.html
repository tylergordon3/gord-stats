Draft DNA

Not how well each manager drafts — that's the Manager Draft Report — but how they draft. Positional timing, early-round shape, and what the championship drafts had in common.

Sample size: 3 drafts, 381 picks in total, so roughly 38 picks per manager. That is enough to describe a habit and not enough to prove one — read the 'never before round N' lines as 'hasn't yet', not as a law. Kickers and defenses are excluded throughout.

Champion Blueprint - how the title winners drafted

Champions taken from Sleeper's playoff bracket, then measured against everyone else who drafted that same year. Starter Hit % is the share of a manager's picks that finished inside their position's weekly starter pool (top 10 QB / 24 RB / 24 WR / 10 TE for a 10-team, 2-flex league).

2025-2026 Champion 13 54% -3.6 -18.3

Across all 3 titles the champion beat the field's hit rate 3 of 3 times, but only led the field in value against ADP 2 of 3 times. On this sample, titles track picks that worked more than picks that looked like bargains on draft day — though three seasons cannot separate that from simple luck.
