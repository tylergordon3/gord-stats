All-Time Injury Impacts

A 'missed game' in this context is defined as a player who was drafted by a team and did not play for the entirety of the game.
Players traded, or picked up on the waiver wire, are counted for the team who originally drafted them (or not at all if not drafted).
Players who did not play or the entire season, such as Joe MIxon who was on IR for the entire 2025-2026 season, are also not considered.
