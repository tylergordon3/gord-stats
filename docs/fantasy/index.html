Not all missed games hurt equally, so each injury is also weighted by how much the player mattered:
High-Impact Games Missed — games missed by players drafted in the first 2 rounds or scoring at weekly-starter pace: top 25% median weekly points among drafted players at their position that season, with at least 4 games played (so a couple of spike weeks don't count as starter production).
Est. Pts Lost — games missed × the player's median weekly score, so losing a stud costs far more than losing a bench stash.
