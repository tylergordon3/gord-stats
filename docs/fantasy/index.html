All-Time Injury Impacts

Eligible players: drafted by a team (accountable all 14 weeks), plus waiver / free-agent pickups held at least 4 weeks — a pickup only answers for games missed while actually on the roster (add week until dropped or traded).
Not eligible: short-term streamers, and pickups of players drafted that season (their missed games are already charged to the drafter).

Not all missed games hurt equally, so each injury is also weighted by how much the player mattered:
High-Impact Games Missed — games missed by players drafted in the first 2 rounds or scoring at weekly-starter pace: top 25% median weekly points among drafted players at their position that season, with at least 5 games played (so a couple of spike weeks don't count as starter production).
Est. Pts Lost — games missed × the player's median weekly score, so losing a stud costs far more than losing a bench stash.
