How to read this page

The Median Tracker ranks every team by points so far (by projection before kickoff), each with its expected final (points plus what its unfinished starters are projected to add), with the median line through the middle; each team is told who it has to stay ahead of or pass, and how many teams would have to pass it (or it has to pass) out of the teams that still can; how many points that takes, and which starters it has left to get them. A row turns solid green or red once a team is locked above or below the median: its ceiling (every starter still to play matching his position's best week in league history) or floor (his position's worst) can no longer change where it finishes against the others' ceilings and floors. Med is each team's margin against the week's median score - the league plays a second game against it every week - live once games are on, on the expected finals before. GS Proj is this site's projection for the week: the power model's points per game for each player, tilted by the market's implied total for his team this week (a defense the other way, on what its opponent is expected to score), and zero on a bye. Beside it, three outside projections for the same week: Slpr is Sleeper's, ESPN is ESPN's, FP is the FantasyPros expert consensus (whose start/sit grade sits by the name); their average is the Consensus the scoreboard compares us against. Pts is the blended projection (grey) - GordStats, Sleeper, ESPN and FantasyPros averaged, so no one source's lean sets it - until a player's game kicks off, then his points with the expected final under them (points plus the unplayed share of the blend), refreshed about once a minute, then final. The Median Tracker and Med run on the same blend; the win bars are GordStats alone. Ahead of the final whistle a roster whose bench out-projects a starter gets the swap spelled out under the table. Rebuilt several times a day and every ten minutes while games are on (last: Sep 14, 9:20 PM EDT); finished weeks stay on record. Season-long standing lives on the power rankings.
