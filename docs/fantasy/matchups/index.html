Weekly Matchups

Zelk Team Fanasty Football — every 2026-2027 matchup with both rosters in full. GS Proj is this site's projection for the week: the power model's points per game for each player, tilted by the market's implied total for his team this week (a defense the other way, on what its opponent is expected to score), and zero on a bye. Beside it, three outside projections for the same week: Slpr is Sleeper's, ESPN is ESPN's, FP is the FantasyPros expert consensus (whose start/sit grade sits by the name); their average is the Consensus the scoreboard compares us against. Pts is what the league has scored so far, with the stat line behind it. Ahead of the final whistle a roster whose bench out-projects a starter gets the swap spelled out under the table. Rebuilt several times a day and every ten minutes while games are on (last: Sep 3, 10:55 PM EDT); finished weeks stay on record. Season-long standing lives on the power rankings.
