Your synced leagues

    Sleeper (NFL)

    The league id is the long number in your league's web address: sleeper.com/leagues/1234567890123456/team. Nothing is asked of your Sleeper account - the league is read through Sleeper's public API.

    Yahoo (college)

    The league key looks like 474.l.21318 - the game code, then .l., then your league id. Yahoo only shares a league its commissioner has set to public; a private one cannot be read without signing in to Yahoo, which this site does not do.

    A league can be re-synced every five minutes. Removing it deletes the row; deleting your account takes every synced league with it.
