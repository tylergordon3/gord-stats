Strength of Schedule & Victory

SOS: Strength of Schedule - Difficulty of Schedule (Learn More)

SOV: Strength of Victory - Combined Win-Loss percentage of defeated opponents

Scoring Luck: Average percentage of an opponent's points scored on you vs their total PF for season.

Luck Example: After week 13, if an opponent scored same amount of points every game, their ratio per game would be 7.69% (~7.69 * 13 = 100)

More Examples: Week 4: 25% | Week 8: 12.5% | Week 12: 8.33% | Week 13: 7.69% | Week 14: 7.14%
