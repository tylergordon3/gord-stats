How it Works

The same model as the college page: one strength number per team plus home field (1.5 points), fitted by ridge regression to every game since 2014, with older games fading on a 180-day half-life. Margin and total are modelled separately; the scores and the win probability fall out of them.

Before this page existed it was scored walk-forward on 1693 games: margin RMSE 13.12, winners 63.7%. The closing line runs about 13.3 and 66-67% over the same years - there are no injuries, no weather and no quarterback news in this model, and the book has all three. Where the two differ by a field goal the card says lean.
